How we work

Every guide is researched and drafted with the help of AI, then put through a second editorial review pass against strict rules:

  • Direct answers first. Each guide opens with a short answer you can act on.
  • No invented numbers. We don't publish made-up statistics, prices, quotes or studies. Product details change, so we tell you what to confirm on the vendor's site.
  • Honest picks. We only recommend a tool where it genuinely fits, and we say who it is not for.
  • Kept fresh. Older guides are re-edited regularly; the "Updated" date on each guide shows when.
